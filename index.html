Main project

ResiWare

Senior capstone · Team of 4 · Sep 2025 – May 2026

A system that detects ransomware and responds on its own. It reads security alerts, groups them into incidents with a local LLM, scores the risk, and takes action.

PythonOllama / Qwen 3 8BhttpxSQLiteAnsiblepytest

How it decides what to do

Fixed rulesAI model

  1. Input

    Options arrive

    The system gets the possible actions for an incident.

  2. Rules

    Policy engine

    Sorts actions by role and disruption cost. Blocks forbidden ones, forces required ones, then ranks the rest.

  3. AI

    AI chooses

    The AI agent picks from the approved list only. Broken JSON output is repaired.

  4. Rules

    Guard

    If the AI picks something not allowed, the guard swaps in the best approved action.

  5. Feedback

    Result reported

    The outcome goes back for retraining. It is saved and resent if the service is down.
